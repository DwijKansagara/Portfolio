Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data is handled when you visit this portfolio, use its optional features, or contact Dwij Kansagara.

Operator: Dwij Kansagara, an individual portfolio operator based in Rajkot, Gujarat, India. Privacy requests can be sent to kansagara.dwij@gmail.com.

1. Data handled by this site

FeatureData and purpose
Basic website deliveryThe hosting provider may process standard request data such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, date, and time to deliver and secure the site. This portfolio does not use that information for advertising or visitor profiling.
Theme preferenceThe site stores only your light or dark theme choice in local storage on your device. It is used to remember the display setting.
ContactThe contact form creates a draft in your email application. Nothing is submitted to this website. If you choose to send the email, Dwij receives the name, email address, and message that you provide so he can reply.
Distance toolYour browser asks for location permission only after you select the distance button. The distance to Rajkot is calculated in your browser. Your coordinates are not sent to this website or stored by it.
Optional on-device assistantQuick answers run without a model download. If you select the download button, model files are requested from Hugging Face and GitHub. Questions and generated answers are held in memory and processed on your device. Those providers receive ordinary network request data when files are downloaded.

2. Data not collected for marketing

This portfolio has no user accounts, advertising pixels, analytics service, newsletter form, payment form, customer counter, review system, or cross-site tracking. It does not sell or rent personal data.

3. Why data is handled

  • To deliver and secure the website.
  • To remember a display preference selected on your device.
  • To respond when you voluntarily send an email.
  • To provide features that you explicitly request, such as distance calculation or the optional on-device assistant.

4. Sharing and processors

Website request data may be handled by the hosting provider, Antideploy, as part of site delivery and security. Email is handled by the sender's email provider and Dwij's email provider. Hugging Face and GitHub receive network requests only if you enable the optional model download or follow an external link. Instagram and GitHub receive a request only when you follow their links.

5. Retention

The site itself does not keep contact-form submissions or location data. Email correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to reply, manage the conversation, or meet a legal obligation, and is normally deleted within 12 months after the conversation ends. Theme preference remains on your device until you clear site data. The assistant conversation remains in page memory until it is cleared, the assistant is closed, or the page is reloaded.

6. Your choices and rights

You can refuse location permission, avoid enabling the assistant, clear local site data in your browser, and choose not to send the email draft. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, updating, or erasure of personal data held in email correspondence, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance by emailing kansagara.dwij@gmail.com.

7. India privacy law

This policy is written to support clear notice, purpose limitation, data minimisation, consent, access, correction, erasure, and grievance handling under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules as they come into force. Requests will be handled according to the law that applies at the time.

8. Children

This general portfolio is not designed to collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent personal data by email, contact Dwij so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

9. Security and external links

Reasonable steps are used to minimise collection and protect the site, but no internet transmission or email system can be guaranteed completely secure. External sites have their own privacy practices.

10. Changes and complaints

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new date. For a question, rights request, or complaint, email kansagara.dwij@gmail.com. Please describe the request clearly so it can be addressed.
